How it works

Three steps to
accurate bends.

01

Pick up your harmonica and play

Open Ratatune and select your key. Then blow or draw any note — the app listens through your device microphone with no extra hardware needed.

02

See the exact cent deviation

The tuner dial and large number show how flat or sharp you are in real time. The needle swings to your actual pitch — no guessing, no lag, just the truth.

03

Adjust, improve, stop guessing

Ratatune's guidance tells you what to change — more breath pressure, looser jaw, stronger bend. Practice with feedback and watch your accuracy trend upward over time.
